The Life of an Owl

Hunting

Owls are silent, nocturnal hunters. Their specially adapted feathers muffle the sound of flight, allowing them to swoop down on prey without being detected until it's too late.

Vision

Owls have enormous eyes relative to their skulls, giving them exceptional night vision. Because their eyes are fixed in place, they've developed the ability to rotate their heads up to 270 degrees.

Hearing

Many owl species have asymmetrical ear placements, with one ear higher than the other. This allows them to pinpoint the exact location of prey using sound alone, even in complete darkness.

Habitat

Owls are found on every continent except Antarctica, living in forests, deserts, grasslands, and even urban areas. Different species have adapted to nearly every environment on Earth.
